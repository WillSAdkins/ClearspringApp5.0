What I took from each

Dark, and photography carries the colour
Passion City runs a full-bleed video loop behind its statement. On a phone that translates to a dark app where your congregation photographs glow rather than sit inside tinted cards. The gradients here stand in for real images so the type can be judged against actual tonal range.
The serif italic word
Passion sets one word of a heading in italic serif — "Select a City", "Around the Passion Movement". It's the whole identity in one move: confident sans, one soft word. Archivo at 800 with Instrument Serif italic does the same job here.
Your own words, not placeholder copy
"Jesus is always the lead story" and "There is a place for you" are from your site. Real service time, real address, real programmes — Bible College, Flashpoint Prayer, Kids Church.
Tiles and rules, no cards
Both references use square photographic tiles and full-width statement bands. Tinted cards with borders are the thing that currently makes the app read as generic.
What I'd flag
This design lives or dies on photography. It needs a small library of good, dark-friendly images — congregation, worship, the building. Without them the tiles are just coloured rectangles. Worth deciding whether you have those before committing.
